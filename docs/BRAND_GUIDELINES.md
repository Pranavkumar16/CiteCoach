# CiteCoach Brand Guidelines

## Brand foundation

**Product name:** CiteCoach  
**Positioning:** A private, evidence-first AI research companion for understanding and verifying information across documents.  
**Primary tagline:** Every answer. Backed by evidence.  
**Supporting line:** Your private AI research companion.

CiteCoach should feel like an editorial research journal: thoughtful, credible, precise and quietly distinctive. Avoid generic AI visual language.

## Ink & Signal palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Guidance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary dark | Oxblood Ink | `#271B21` | Dark surfaces, navigation, headings |
| Deep background | Night Ink | `#1A1117` | Dark app background |
| Warm canvas | Warm Parchment | `#F1E9D8` | Main light background |
| Paper surface | Chalk Paper | `#FBF8F1` | Cards and raised light surfaces |
| Signature accent | Acid Citron | `#D9EF57` | Primary actions and selected states |
| Evidence accent | Signal Vermilion | `#D85B43` | Citation markers, evidence highlights, warnings where appropriate |
| Muted neutral | Muted Stone | `#B7A99A` | Secondary borders and subdued elements |

### Colour rules

- Use Oxblood Ink and Warm Parchment as the main visual foundation.
- Reserve Acid Citron for intentional interactive emphasis. Do not use it for long text.
- Use Signal Vermilion to draw attention to citations, evidence references and important distinctions.
- Use dark ink text on citron surfaces for legibility.
- Avoid blue/purple AI gradients, neon glow, glassmorphism and excessive gradients.
- Keep semantic success, warning and error states distinguishable from brand decoration.

## Product voice

- Clear, direct and grounded.
- Prefer “Show evidence” over “AI magic”.
- State uncertainty explicitly.
- Never imply a claim is verified unless the cited source supports it.
- Describe offline and privacy capabilities only where the current implementation guarantees them.

## UI principles

1. **Evidence is navigable:** citations should open the exact source passage or page whenever possible.
2. **The document remains central:** reading, citations and chat should feel connected.
3. **Calm hierarchy:** use typography, spacing and subtle rules rather than decorative gradients.
4. **Accessible contrast:** verify text, icons and controls in both Daylight and dark themes.
5. **Consistent components:** use `AppThemeData` for theme-dependent colours instead of hard-coded legacy palette values.

## Naming and typography

- Always spell the brand as **CiteCoach**.
- Use title case for the product name.
- Keep the tagline sentence case: **Every answer. Backed by evidence.**
- The current in-app typeface is Lexend. Keep body copy highly readable and avoid overly condensed weights.

## Do / don't

**Do:** editorial layouts, warm neutrals, strong evidence links, restrained citron accents, clear page references.

**Don't:** generic blue-purple gradients, glowing AI orbs, decorative neon, unsupported privacy claims, untraceable answer claims.
